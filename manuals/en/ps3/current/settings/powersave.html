Power Save Settings

Adjust settings for the power save feature. To save power, the PS3™ system and wireless controllers can be set to turn off automatically after a certain period of inactivity.

System Auto-Off

After 20 Minutes
After 1 Hour
After 2 Hours
After 3 Hours
After 4 Hours
After 5 Hours		 The system turns off after the specified period of inactivity.
Off The system is not set to turn off automatically.

Hints

  • You can set the amount of time until the system turns off for [Video/TV/Music/Photo] and for [Game/Other Features].
  • The system will not turn off automatically when background download is being performed. When the download is complete, the system will turn off automatically.
