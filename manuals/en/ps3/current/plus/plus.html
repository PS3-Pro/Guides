About PlayStation®Plus

PlayStation®Plus is a membership service for PSNSM.
PlayStation®Plus membership can be purchased from PlayStation®Store. As a PlayStation®Plus member, you can enjoy special pricing on some content, automatic downloads and installations of game update data and other added features.


Hints

  • Content and types of services vary depending on the country or region. For details, visit the SIE Web site for your region.
  • For details about the automatic update feature, see (Settings) > (System Settings) > [Automatic Update] on this guide.

Subscribing to PlayStation®Plus

You can purchase a PlayStation®Plus subscription service membership from PlayStation®Store, in the same way you purchase other items. Upon purchase of a PlayStation®Plus subscription, the PlayStation®Plus logo will be displayed next to your avatar in the XMB™ menu, as well as with features or PlayStation®Store content exclusive to PlayStation®Plus.


Hint

To cancel the automatic renewal of the PlayStation®Plus subscription service, select [Cancel Automatic Renewal] under (PlayStation™Network) > (Account Management) > (Transaction Management) > [Services List] > [PlayStation®Plus].
