Using Photo Gallery

Photo Gallery is an application that enhances the experience of viewing photos saved on the PS3™ system. You can group photos in various ways such as by date and time, by event or by the number of people in the photos. You can also create a playlist of selected photos and select a photo frame for the playlist. For details on how to use Photo Gallery, select here.

Starting Photo Gallery

To use Photo Gallery, you must first download and install the Photo Gallery application. Select (Photo Gallery) under (Photo) to download the application. Follow the on-screen instructions to complete the installation. Photo Gallery will start automatically after it is installed.

Hint

To install Photo Gallery, you must have at least 110 MB of free space in the system storage of the PS3™ system.

Deleting Photo Gallery

You can delete Photo Gallery from the system storage. Select (Photo Gallery) under (Photo), press the button, and then select [Delete] from the options menu.

Hint

Even if the application is deleted, the (Photo Gallery) icon will remain on the XMB™ screen. If you select the icon, Photo Gallery will be downloaded again.
