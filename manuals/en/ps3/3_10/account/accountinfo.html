Managing account information

To use this feature, you may be required to update the system software.


Check or edit information for PlayStation®Network accounts. You must be signed in to PlayStation®Network to perform these operations. Select (Account Management) under (PlayStation®Network).

Items that can be checked or edited
Account Information

You can check or edit information related to your PlayStation®Network accounts.

  • Billing Information
  • Sign-In ID (E-mail Address)
  • Password
  • Name
  • Address
  • Avatar
  • Profile
  • Notification Preferences
Sub Account Management

You can review or edit settings for an associated sub account.

  • Parental Control
  • Sub Account Password
Redeem Codes You can redeem a PlayStation®Network Card number or promotion code.
Transaction Management

You can add funds to the wallet that is used for PlayStation®Network services such as PlayStation®Store, or check usage history for various services.

  • Add Funds
  • Require Password at Checkout
  • Automatic Funding
  • Transaction History
  • Download List
  • Services List
System Activation You can activate or deactivate the PS3™ system or the PSP™ system that you use for the account.
Facebook

If you have a Facebook® account, you can adjust settings related to sharing information on Facebook.

  • Share trophy information
  • Share information about content you have purchased in PlayStation®Store
  • Share game event information
  • Delete Facebook account information‎ from the PS3™ system

Hints

  • Items that you can check or edit vary depending on the type of PlayStation®Network account and on the country or region of use. For details, contact the technical support line for your region.
