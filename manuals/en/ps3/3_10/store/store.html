PlayStation®Store overview

To use this feature, you may be required to update the system software.


(PlayStation®Store) is an online shop within PlayStation®Network where you can download  (as a purchase or for free) products such as games, additional items for games, or video content.


(1)

Switch stores

Game Store of PlayStation®Store You can download games and additional items for games.
Video Store of PlayStation®Store * You can purchase video content such as full-length movies or TV shows. You can also rent content that has time restrictions on playback.

*  The Video Store of PlayStation®Store is only available in a limited number of countries and regions. Languages that are supported are also limited and the types of services available vary depending on the region. For details, contact the technical support line for your region.

(2)

Control buttons

Search Search for content in PlayStation®Store.
Redeem Codes Displays the screen for entering a PlayStation®Network Card number or a promotion code. By entering the information, you can add funds to the wallet for your account or exchange a promotion code for goods.
View Downloads Displays a list of content items from PlayStation®Store. You can re-download content items that were downloaded in the past.*
View Cart Displays a list of products that are in your shopping cart. You can proceed to the checkout screen from here.

*  Some items cannot be downloaded more than once.

(3)

View Mode
Changes how items are displayed.

(4)

Products that can be downloaded (as a purchase or for free) such as games or video content

(5)

The avatar and online ID for the PlayStation®Network account you used to sign in *

*  These items will be displayed only if you have a PlayStation®Network account.

(6)


Items that are currently in your cart

(7)


Items you have purchased that are available for re-purchase.

(8)


Items you have purchased that are not available for re-purchase.*

*  You can find these items on your download list.


Hints

  • PlayStation®Network and (PlayStation®Store) are available only in certain regions and languages. Also, types of products and contents that are provided in (PlayStation®Store) vary depending on the country or region of residence. For details, contact the technical support line for your region.
  • To download products (as a purchase or for free) from (PlayStation®Store), you must create a PlayStation®Network account and accept a user agreement. You can create an account in (Sign Up for PlayStation®Network) under (PlayStation®Network).
