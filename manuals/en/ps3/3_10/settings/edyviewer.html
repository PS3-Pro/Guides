Settings > Edy Viewer

Edy Viewer

This setting is only available on PS3™ systems sold in Japan.


Connect to Edy Center to receive Edy Gifts, check your online balance or make use of other services. A contactless IC card reader / writer ("PaSoRi", sold separately) is required. You must first accept the user agreement that is displayed on the screen.

(Edy Gifts) Receive Edy Gifts.

Edy Gifts is a type of Edy service that can be used on the Internet. Receive Edy (e-money) provided as Edy Gifts by participating companies.
(Checking online balance) Check the balance of Edys detected on the Edy Center server.
(Reader / Writer Self-Diagnosis) Check the status, version number or other information for a contactless IC card reader / writer ("PaSoRi", sold separately) connected to the PS3™ system.

Hints

  • You cannot add funds to an Edy using the PS3™ system. Note also that you cannot use Edy for online shopping on most Web sites.
  • To use Edy for online shopping at (PlayStation®Store), you must first add funds to your wallet. For details on adding funds, see （PlayStation®Network) > (PlayStation®Store) > [Wallet] in this guide. You cannot make purchases directly using Edy.
  • For more information on Edy, visit http://www.edy.jp/.
