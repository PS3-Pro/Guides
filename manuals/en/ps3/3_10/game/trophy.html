Trophy Collection

You can earn trophies when you accomplish certain objectives in games that support the trophy feature.

Types of trophies

Trophies are categorized into four grades. In general, grades are based on the level of difficulty in earning the trophies. The types of trophies available and the conditions by which you can earn trophies vary depending on the game.

Checking your trophy collection

1.

Select (Game) > (Trophy Collection).
Trophy achievement rates for the games are displayed.

2.

Select the game that you want to check.
Information is displayed for the trophies that are available in the game. Trophy images are displayed for the trophies that you have earned.

Saving your trophy information on the PlayStation®Network server

If you create a PlayStation®Network account, you can save information about the trophies you have earned on the PlayStation®Network server. You can also view your trophy collection in the [Profile] screen under (Friends) and compare trophy earning status with Friends.


Trophy information is automatically saved on the PlayStation®Network server under the following conditions:

  • When you select (Game) > (Trophy Collection) when signed in to PlayStation®Network
  • When you select (Compare Trophies) in the [Profile] screen under (Friends)
